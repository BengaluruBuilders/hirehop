Construction

Hand-drawn cubic curves, no primitive circles or trapezoids. Heads tilt 4–10°, shoulders sit off level, limbs are tapered sleeves built on a bent spine. Outline varies 2.5–3.5 dp: silhouettes 3.4, features 2.8–3.0, hair strands and creases 2.2–2.5.

Palette

Ink #16181D, skin #C98B62 with flat shade #B0764F, hair #3E2A22 so inner ink strands read. Woman: #FFC94D top, denim #4F6FA3. Man: jade #0B7A5C shirt over a white tee. Flat fills, no gradients.

Where it lives

Home hero, empty, error, offline, scanned PDF, export done, delete account. Never on working screens: gap analysis, bullet review, editors, lists with data.

Dark theme

Spots sit on a 180 dp tint disc (#DDF2EA) so the ink outline stays legible. The home hero keeps the jade header behind it. Same vectors, no redraw.

Compose

One ImageVector per pose, 40–70 paths (budget 120). Plain fills and strokes only: no blur, masks or clip paths. Rasterise once into a cached ImageBitmap per size, so 60 fps holds on a 3 GB phone.

Gesture first

Each spot tells its state without text: shrug (empty), raised palm (error), frown at phone (offline), squint at a photo page (scanned), both arms up (exported), wave (goodbye). Only export-done may use motion.hop.
