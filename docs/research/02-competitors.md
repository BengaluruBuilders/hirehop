# TailorMyResume Research 02: Competitor Landscape

Date of research: 2026-09-30.
Author: research agent 2 of 6.
Scope: web AI resume tools, Indian job platforms, platform AI, and Android apps on Google Play.

## 0. How to read this report

- Source grades: A = official pricing page or store listing. B = reputable press. C = forum, anecdote, or affiliate blog.
- Most pricing pages change often. Treat all prices as valid on the fetch date only. Check again before launch.
- The Play Store data was fetched on 2026-09-30 with the India storefront (`gl=IN`, `hl=en`). Prices are in INR.
- The web search budget of this session ended (200 of 200 calls) before the research was finished. Some items rely on search snippets (grade C) or have no data. The report marks each gap.
- Cells marked "?" mean "not confirmed from any source". Cells marked "No" mean "not found in the listing or feature page". This is not proof of absence.
- Review complaints come from the 1-star and 2-star review feed of each Play listing. They are anecdotes (grade C for the pattern, grade A for the fact that the review exists). The 1-star feed is biased toward complaints.

## 1. Executive summary

1. The market is crowded with resume builders. It is thin in the area that TailorMyResume targets: one saved profile that feeds honest, per-job tailoring, on a phone, at Indian prices.
2. The web tools (Teal, Huntr, Careerflow, Kickresume, Rezi) already do JD match, tailoring, cover letters, and a tracker. They are desktop-first and priced in USD (USD 9 to USD 49 per month). Their Android presence is weak or absent.
3. The top Android apps by installs are template builders. Their strongest common complaint is the paywall at download time and surprise charges. AI tailoring against a JD is rare in these apps.
4. The Indian incumbents have huge distribution (Naukri 50M+ installs, apna 50M+, Indeed 100M+, LinkedIn 1B+). Naukri launched an AI Resume Maker on 2025-11-21. Internshala, Naukri Campus, and apna give free resume builders to freshers. Free is the price anchor for TailorMyResume's target user.
5. Indeed Career Scout is the closest platform product to TailorMyResume's idea (AI coach in a mobile app that tailors the resume). It is US, Canada, and UK only as of the last source (Oct 2025, grade C). Indeed expanded its ChatGPT app to India on 2026-05-01 (grade C, trade press).
6. No competitor has a clear "we never invent qualifications" guardrail as a product feature. LinkedIn warns users to check its AI output for accuracy. One Play review of Sprout says the app changed the user's name on a resume without consent. This is the white space for an honesty guardrail, and also a trust risk that every AI tool shares.

## 5. White-space analysis

What is served well (do not compete head-on):
- Template libraries and PDF export. Very crowded. Free options exist (Internshala, Naukri Campus, apna).
- ATS keyword scoring on the web. Jobscan, Rezi, and others own this.
- Mass job lists. Naukri, apna, LinkedIn, Indeed own this.

What is not served well:

1. Mobile-native JD-to-application flow. On Android, the top builders are template tools. The web tools that do JD tailoring have no real Android app (Teal, Careerflow, Rezi not found on Play). Huntr and Simplify apps are tiny or broken.
2. Honest tailoring. No competitor states a guardrail such as "we only reword what you did". Rezi and ResumeGyani use outcome claims. LinkedIn only warns users to verify. One Sprout review alleges unauthorized resume changes. A visible "every claim maps to your profile" design is unclaimed.
3. Saved profile reuse. One Kickresume review says the AI writer "does not remember anything about you". Intelligent CV users praise profile copy. Users want one profile that drives many applications. Web tools have it but keep it in the desktop flow.
4. Fresher needs. Two Play reviews say builders do not help people with little experience. Indian freshers have projects, internships, coursework, and college activities. Tools do not guide them to turn these into bullets.
5. India pricing and payment behavior. Web tools price in USD by week or month. Play apps use surprise weekly or renewal charges. Reviews show anger at hidden fees and refund refusals. A clear INR price, a one-time "application pack", and a visible refund path fits the review pain.
6. Saved application workspace on a phone. Play trackers have under 1K installs. Users track in Excel or Notion. A tracker that stores each application's documents, prep questions, notes, and status is not a strong native product today.
7. Export quality. Multiple reviews say the PDF is an image, has broken links, or cannot be uploaded. A clean, text-selectable PDF and DOCX is a real, checkable differentiator.
8. Language. apna supports Hinglish for mock interviews. Resume tools are English only in most cases. Hindi UI is a possible later gap, not an MVP need.

Fastest copier: Naukri. It has the profile, the traffic, the payments, and an AI resume maker already in market. Its weakness is trust and neutrality. TailorMyResume must not fight it on job listings.

Second: Indeed Career Scout, if launched in India. It is free and mobile.

Real long-run risk: free general chatbots plus the Play Store reflex of "free". TailorMyResume must be better than a raw prompt at three things: it remembers the profile, it refuses to invent, and it saves and exports the application set.

## 7. Implications for the PRD

Keep as differentiators:
1. Saved profile as the single source of truth for every document. Show the user which profile item supports each tailored line.
2. "Never invent" rule as a visible product feature: flag unsupported requirements as gaps instead of filling them. Do not promise ATS scores or outcomes. Rezi and Wonsulting claims make this a safe, distinct position.
3. Android-first flow: import resume, paste JD, see gaps, edit, export, save. Keep it working on low-end phones and slow networks.
4. Saved application workspace (documents, prep questions, notes, status).
5. Clean, text-selectable PDF and DOCX export.
6. India-first price in INR with a transparent, one-time "application pack". No weekly auto-renew. State refund terms on the paywall.

Add:
- Fresher support: prompts that turn projects, internships, college roles, and coursework into evidence lines. A "no experience yet" path.
- Clear pricing screen before the user does the work. Sprout and builders that reveal the price at the last step draw the most anger.
- Free preview of the export before the paywall, with no watermark trick that blocks the file.
- Cancel and refund inside the app.
- Import from an existing resume PDF and from a Naukri or LinkedIn profile export (a manual paste is enough for the MVP).

Cut or delay:
- Any "ATS score" number as a headline. Competitors use it. It invites outcome claims that TailorMyResume must not make. If shown, label it as a keyword coverage check, not a pass rate.
- Job discovery. Naukri, apna, Indeed, and LinkedIn win here. It is already a "later" item. Keep it later.
- Live interview copilot features. They conflict with the honesty stance and Final Round AI, Interview Pilot, and OfferGoose already occupy them. Voice mock interviews stay a later, prep-only feature.
- Auto-apply. It has weak trust in reviews.
- A large template library. Ten clean ATS-safe templates are enough. Users do not need more.

Pricing hints (hypotheses, not validated):
- Free anchors: Internshala, Naukri Campus, apna free resume. TailorMyResume needs a free tier that shows the gap analysis and one tailored document.
- Paid anchors: Naukri Pro INR 999 per month; Resume.io INR 249 per week or INR 1,999 per year; ResumeGyani INR 299 per week-plan; Kickresume Play INR 520 to INR 2,250. A one-time application pack under about INR 199 to INR 499 sits below the anchors. The active-job-search plan can sit near INR 299 to INR 999 per month. Validate with a price test.

Open questions to resolve before the PRD is final:
1. Does Naukri Pro's resume maker tailor to a JD? The press release does not say so. Test the product.
2. Does Indeed Career Scout run in India now? The source is dated Oct 2025.
3. What do Teal+, Jobscan, and Resume Worded charge now? The official pages were not readable.
4. Is there a Careerflow, Teal, or Rezi iOS app that a fresher would use? Not checked.
5. Real conversion and retention of the top Play builders (needs a store intelligence tool such as Sensor Tower or data.ai).
